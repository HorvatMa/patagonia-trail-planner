## A. Candidates

### Las Horquetas to Camping Río Turbio, one night out and back — verdict: ADD* (confirm that CONAF accepts an out-and-back and the current ticket price)
- GPT ref: first part of the GPT32 Cerro Castillo circuit, which is the classic Las Horquetas to Villa Cerro Castillo trek [manual p.1229, p.417]. The GPT32 regular route itself starts at Villa Frei and is Difficulty 5/5, so it is not used here [manual p.742]. This is the standard park trek's day 1, not a GPT-only route.
- Car access: Las Horquetas Grandes, the U-bend on the Carretera Austral about 70 km from Coyhaique, roughly 7.5 km south of the Laguna Chiguay ranger post [web, Wikiexplora trekking page, undated; the text quotes 2024 fees]. Paved/graded Ruta 7, no 4x4 needed. Coordinates: car about -45.9805, -71.9554 (OSM "Las Horquetas", Coyhaique). Río Turbio camp about -45.982, -72.122 (OSM "Río Turbio"; the camp is on the left bank a little past the river, so this is approximate). OSM and the manual agree about where the trek starts. I could not find the exact trailhead pin on OSM, so please check it on the first day against a GPX (see Confidence).
- Hike: one night, out and back. About 15–17 km each way (15.3 km per Ruta de los Parques; 16 km and 17 km on other sites) and 5–7 h each way, so about 31 km total over two days (day 1 and day 2 each about 15 km, below the 25 km day limit). Ascent per day: [estimate] about 500–700 m. The whole circuit is 2,334 m up, but day 1 is the gentle first stage and its own figure is unpublished. There is also a CONAF campground about 1–1.5 h before Río Turbio if energy runs out [web, Wikiexplora].
- Terrain: 4x4 track first, then clear trail through lenga forest and cattle meadows. Red and white marks. Five unbridged streams, some iron-coloured; depending on the season they are crossed on logs or stones, or barefoot with water "up to the knee" [web, Wikiexplora]. In Jan–Feb flows should be lower than in spring, but the knee-deep ford limit is borderline: bring sandals, and turn back if a crossing is above the knee. Gates with ladders ("don't open the gates"). No exposure or snow on this stretch. Grade T2, hazard tags: ford, private-land (the trail crosses ranch properties, but it is part of the official ticketed trek, so no separate permission is needed [web, Wikiexplora and a trip summary]).
- Fees / permits: park ticket; the Wikiexplora page quotes 14,000 CLP Chilean / 16,000 CLP foreigner (2024 figures), and the earlier report found about 20,000–21,250 CLP at the Villa Cerro Castillo side in 2025/26. Assume about 20,000 CLP (about 20 EUR) per person plus camping fee [estimate]. Pay online (pasesparques.cl or senderospncc.cl) or at the ranger post. Register with CONAF at the entrance; the entrance is open 07:00–13:00 [web, trip report]. Camping at Río Turbio is on the official CONAF site (ranger hut, tables, latrine).
- Current status: a Feb 2026 Komoot comment on the nearby viewpoint mentions a second ticket checkpoint, so enforcement is active [web 2026-02]. I found no dated closure for Río Turbio. Campamento El Bosque (beyond Río Turbio) is closed to camping (earlier report), which only affects through-trekkers.
- Why it's worth it: it is the quiet end of the park, with no trekker queue on the way to the Laguna and a real overnight in the forest with valley views. It uses one of the two optional overnight slots without needing a multi-day trek.
- Fits: plans are not written yet. Best slot is between two Carretera Austral transit days, with the car left at Las Horquetas (it is on the Balmaceda–Cerro Castillo road, about 1 h 15 from the airport). It needs a flex day before and after if the weather turns.
- Confidence: medium-low on distances and ascent (no GPX, sources differ by 2 km), medium on access. Open questions: (1) whether CONAF lets you walk in and out the same way, and under what ticket; (2) the exact trailhead; (3) the knee-deep fords at the actual flow.

### Sendero Laguna Chiguay — verdict: MAYBE (short filler only)
- GPT ref: none. Park trail on the Carretera Austral next to the GPT32 route.
- Car access: Laguna Chiguay ranger post and campground, about -45.9838, -71.8765 (OSM "Laguna Chiguay": agrees). On the Carretera Austral, about 57 km from Coyhaique. Normal car.
- Hike: 4 km loop, about 1.5 h, easy, open all year [web, rutadelosparques.org, 2018 copyright; undated]. Ascent not stated, but it is a lagoon walk, so probably under 100 m [estimate].
- Terrain: T1, forest and lagoon.
- Fees: ranger-post pass (amount unconfirmed). Camping there is a paid campground. Some reports describe a private campground with a hot shower too (iOverlander, undated).
- Why: only a short stop on the drive south, and the "registration and ticket" post for Las Horquetas. It is not quiet in peak season.
- Fits: any Carretera Austral drive day. Confidence: medium.

### Laguna Verde, Lago Lapparent and the X-727 road — verdict: NO as a hike, useful only as a car camp
- GPT ref: GPT33H km 0–24 [manual p.762], a gravel road with "several nice spots to pitch a tent partly on the shore of lakes" and water from side streams.
- OSM points: Laguna Verde -46.1659, -72.3079; Lago Lapparent -46.2343, -72.1915 (OSM agrees with the manual's names).
- It is road walking on a gravel road, not a hike. The earlier report found a locked gate and two fords past the last house (about 2 h before the Avellano trailhead). The road is private farmland nearby. iOverlander lists a free wild-camp spot at Laguna Verde (undated; the page would not open for me).
- Conclusion: nice quiet car night near Villa Cerro Castillo, but no hike value. Confirm road status and gates locally.

### Salto del Río Ibáñez / Valle Ibáñez drive (X-723) — verdict: NO as a hike, drive-by only
- OSM: Salto del Río Ibáñez -46.2550, -71.9956; mirador -46.2578, -71.9949 (agrees with names, on the Península Levicán road). X-723 links Cerro Castillo and Puerto Ibáñez, with views of Lago Tamango, the Cerro Castillo massif and the falls [web, rutadelosparques.org, undated]. It is a good quiet scenic drive, not a hike. The Paredón de las Manos (rock art, 5,000 years) is a short visit south of the village, with a fee of unknown amount [web 2026-11].

## B. Notes for the existing plans
- Fee ranges for the Cerro Castillo Laguna trail differ by source (14,000–16,000 CLP in the 2024 Wikiexplora text; 20,000–21,250 CLP in 2025/26 sources). Budget about 21,000 CLP per person and confirm the 2026/27 figures at pasesparques.cl or senderospncc.cl.
- Park entrances: Las Horquetas 07:00–13:00 (registered); Villa Cerro Castillo 08:00–12:00 per the earlier report.
- Komoot lists the Laguna Cerro Castillo loop (22.2 km, 1,240 m) as "hard"; do not combine it with other sections on the same day.
- Distance Villa Cerro Castillo to Balmaceda is about 1 h 15 by the Carretera Austral, and about 2.5 h via X-723 and Puerto Ibáñez [web, rutadelosparques.org]. The road south of Cerro Castillo is gravel; carry a spare tire; no petrol station in the village.
- Levicán is served by a subsidised bus only on Mondays and Fridays; the private transfer costs about 35,000 CLP (manual p.761). This is irrelevant for a car trip, but means the Levicán road is quiet.
- The Kayak/Monreal and Horquetas Option 1 area has no usable day hike (see above).
