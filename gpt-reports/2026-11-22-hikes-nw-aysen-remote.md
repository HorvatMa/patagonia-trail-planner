## A. Candidates (best first)

**1. Sendero Laguna Los Pumas (Queulat, Angostura sector, north of Puyuhuapi): ADD***
- Trailhead and access: starts from a small pull-out at the Lago Risopatrón campsites, on the Carretera Austral about 12 km north of Puyuhuapi. The road is gravel, 2WD is fine, and the Carretera Austral is partly paved here. From Coyhaique it is about 4.5 h and 230 km [estimate]. The CONAF ranger house is at Angostura [web 2026-01].
- Distance, ascent, time: about 10 km return, about 600 m, 4–6 h. Sources conflict: one says 6.5 mi and 6 h return, another says 10 km and 4–5 h [web 2026]. The ascent is my [estimate]. Lago Risopatrón sits at about 180 m and the lagoon is probably 700–800 m.
- Grade: T3. It climbs hard through forest with roots and mud, so tag steep-loose. I found no exposure or fords.
- Fees and booking: Queulat requires an advance CONAF ticket, and Angostura may need its own sector selection (the Ventisquero area is the one in the booking system). Hours are reported as Tue–Sun 09:00–14:30 [web 2026, undated]. Check with the CONAF Puyuhuapi office. The park reopened fully in January 2026 after the 2025 works [web 2026-01].
- Crowds: low to moderate. It is in Queulat, but nearly all visitors go to the Ventisquero Colgante. I expect few people here [estimate].
- Car sleeping: CONAF campsites at Lago Risopatrón (fee). Wild pull-outs along the lake are possible but unconfirmed. Puyuhuapi village has hospedajes and campings.
- Why: it is a forest and lake climb that avoids the famous trail. It is the best-documented quiet option in the region.
- Confidence: medium on the route's existence, low on the numbers. OSM has a "Laguna Los Pumas" node at about -44.2290, -72.5660 and "Lago Risopatrón" at -44.2422, -72.5097, so OSM agrees the lagoon is west of the lake. The trailhead point is my estimate.

**2. Lago Verde to La Tapera road-and-track section, first 20–22 km out and back (GPT28H): MAYBE, private-land**
- Access: Lago Verde village sits near the Argentine border. A minor gravel road runs about 80 km from Villa Amengual or La Tapera in the Cisnes valley [estimate]. It is probably 5–6 h from Coyhaique. A 4x4 is wise, and construction trucks and a mine camp were reported in 2025 [web 2025-12]. A bus runs from Coyhaique on Fridays [web 2025-12].
- Distance, ascent, time: about 20–25 km return as an out-and-back from Lago Verde to a camp near km 13 or 22 [web 2025-12]. Ascent is about 300–600 m [estimate], and it takes 6–8 h. The section's difficulty is rated 2/5 and attraction 1/5 [manual p.712]. Forest, plateau and lake views.
- Grade: T2 on minor roads and tracks. Hazards are ford (calf to knee at km 13.3 [web 2025-12]), navigation and private-land.
- Concern: a stretch at km 6–10 needed landowner permission, though hikers had no problems [web 2025-12]. A gate at km 36 is outside the day range. The user's rule says avoid private land, so this is MAYBE only.
- Crowds: very low, since only a few GPT hikers pass.
- Car sleeping: wild pull-outs near Lago Verde village. It has a shop, a hostal and a plaza, and nothing else.
- Confidence: low-medium. Coordinates: Lago Verde about -44.24, -71.85 (OSM ambiguous, with several matches).

**3. Overnight option: Lago Verde plateau camp, km 13 to km 22, back next day: MAYBE, private-land**
- Same trail as 2, with a tent. Water is available about every 2 km and there is little wind shelter on the plateau [web 2025-12]. About 26–44 km over two days [estimate].
- Tábanos (horseflies) are bad on the plateau in Nov and mosquitoes near the lakes in Dec [web 2025-12], so check Feb conditions. Same permission issue.
- Confidence: low.

**4. Sendero Chucao and coast loop, Puerto Raúl Marín Balmaceda: ADD* (short, a rest-day walk)**
- Access: it is a road-end village. The turn-off is at La Junta, then about 70 km of gravel on the X-12, 1.5–2 h [estimate]. From Coyhaique it is about 6–7 h [estimate]. There is also a ferry to Puerto Cisnes [manual, GPT28P].
- Distance, ascent, time: about 3.7 km return (2.3 mi), almost flat, 1.5–2 h [web 2026].
- Grade: T1, no hazards. The Wikiloc track runs "to meet the sea". There are also beaches and a cemetery side trail.
- Crowds: very low, and the village is a quiet fishing hamlet.
- Car sleeping: the village has campings and cabins. Wild camping near the beach is likely tolerated, but confirm locally.
- Confidence: medium. OSM confirms the trail, the "Sendero Chucao" node at -43.7685, -72.9597, close to Puerto Raúl Marín Balmaceda at -43.7771, -72.9521.

## B. Notes
- Region-wide: remote and low-service. Cash is needed. Fuel is available in La Junta, Puyuhuapi and Puerto Cisnes. Fill up before Lago Verde and La Tapera. Check CONAF office hours on Mondays.
- Wild camping: Carretera Austral pull-outs near rivers and lakes are common. Camp on public land only. Insects are bad in Jan–Feb.
- Bases: La Junta (shops, fuel), Puyuhuapi (hospedajes, camping, near Los Pumas), Puerto Cisnes (services).
- Catalogue: already has queulat_colgante, bosque, yelcho and futa. I excluded these and the Pumalín hikes.
- Needs a human step: pull GPX tracks for Los Pumas and the Lago Verde road section, and confirm the Angostura sector rules by phone with CONAF.
