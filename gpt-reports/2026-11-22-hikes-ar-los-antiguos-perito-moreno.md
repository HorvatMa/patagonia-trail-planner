## A. Candidates (best first)

**1. Cerro León from Estancia La Oriental / El Rincón (PN Perito Moreno), day. Verdict: ADD\***
- Access: PN Perito Moreno is remote. The routes are:
  - RP37 gravel from Gobernador Gregores, about 290 km [web 2024-10, Río Negro];
  - RP41 from Lago Posadas, 4x4 only, high pass, usually passable Nov to Apr [web 2021, La Nación; web 2024-10, Río Negro];
  - from Chile Chico via Paso Roballos and Bajo Caracoles to Lago Posadas, then RP41. The drive is a full day (about 6 to 7 h from Chile Chico) [estimate].
- Trailhead: Estancia La Oriental / Centro Operativo (OSM -47.8045, -72.0941). Summit: Cerro León (OSM -47.7791, -72.1338).
- Length: OSM foot-routing gives 6.3 km one way from La Oriental (snaps 12 m and 1 m), so ≈12.5 km return. Guides say 2 to 2.5 h up [web, Moon and Welcome Argentina, undated]. Ascent is ≈600 m [estimate]. Time 4.5 to 5.5 h.
- Grade: T3 (conservative; I found no official grade). Hazards: navigation, steep-loose. Wind is strong. The park says to be back before dark.
- Fees and booking: all visitors must register at the Centro Operativo Clemente Onelli (09:00 to 19:00) [web 2026, argentina.gob.ar]. For a day walk no booking is needed. For the car, camping and refugios are by email, reserve about 1 month ahead [web 2024-10]. The APN entry fee for foreigners is NOT confirmed; ask when booking.
- Crowd: very low, with no shops and no buses. Wild-camping for the car is not allowed in the park (see B).
- Why: guanacos, lakes and views over the Belgrano/Volcán lakes, in one of Argentina's emptiest parks. Confidence: medium.

**2. Península Belgrano circuit (Mirador Lago Belgrano / Sendero Roca Azul), day. Verdict: ADD\* (small circuit) / MAYBE (large circuit)**
- Small circuit: 9.6 km, medium, 3 to 4 h. Large circuit: 16.8 km, "high difficulty", 5 to 6 h, with four refugios on the peninsula [web 2024-10, Río Negro].
- Trailhead: same area as La Oriental (OSM -47.8045, -72.0941). Miradores Belgrano are at -47.876, -72.095 and -47.889, -72.080. The exact trailhead is unconfirmed; ask at the Centro Operativo.
- Ascent ≈400 to 700 m [estimate].
- Grade: T3 (conservative). Hazards: navigation. I saw no exposure reports. Check the large circuit with rangers; the "high difficulty" label might hide scrambling.
- Camping: Camping Agreste "El Arroyo" at -47.865, -72.183 (OSM). It could turn this into a car-supported 1-night trip, but booking is required.
- Fees: as in item 1. Confidence: medium (distances official, grading mine).

**3. Mirador Lago Volcán, day (short). Verdict: ADD**
- Park the car 3 km from El Rincón and walk 5 km to the lake shore [web, Welcome Argentina, undated]. The mirador is at OSM -47.7721, -72.2079, on RP37.
- ≈10 km return, ≈150 m, 2.5 to 3 h. T1 to T2. No special hazards.
- Combine with Cerro León for a two-hike stay. Confidence: medium.

**4. Laguna de los Témpanos (Cerro San Lorenzo sector), day. Verdict: MAYBE**
- Sector of the highest peak in Santa Cruz. The park's 11.5 km circuit (Gilberto refugio, Kris y Doug refugio and domos) is high difficulty, 4 to 5 h. The lagoon is 5.2 km further (2 to 3 h), with one river crossing [web 2024-10].
- Camping Agreste / Domos at OSM -47.628, -72.158. OSM mirador at -47.6145, -72.2102. Foot-routing from camp to mirador is 5.07 km, which matches the stated 5.2 km. That gives ≈10 to 11 km return, ≈400 m [estimate].
- Grade: T3. Hazards: ford (the depth is not stated, so confirm it is below the knee in Jan/Feb), navigation. This is not a hike to the San Lorenzo summit.
- Access: it is about 20 km from the Centro de Informes (RP37 sector). It is far from the Posadas side. Verify the road with rangers.
- Booking: domos and refugios cost money in 2026-27 (see B). Confidence: low to medium.

**5. Cerro La Calle and Laguna Coa (Parque Patagonia, Portal La Ascensión), day. Verdict: ADD**
- Trailhead: Estancia La Ascensión (OSM -46.6072, -71.4075), 17 km from Los Antiguos on RP43 (gravel, 2WD ok) [web 2020 and 2026, Parque Patagonia pages]. Drive about 25 to 30 min from Los Antiguos.
- Official figure: 10.5 km, medium, about 4 h [web, patagoniaandina, updated 2023-06]. OSM shows 8.9 km to Laguna Coa (-46.6684, -71.4843), so it may be a loop or an out-and-back. In the worst case it is ≈20 km return, still within limits. Ascent ≈400 to 500 m [estimate].
- Grade: T2. Hazards: navigation (light). Exposure and scramble: none reported.
- Parque Patagonia land is now a national park (since 2019), so there is no estancia permission issue. Free entry and sign-in at the portal [web 2025-10, turismodebolsillo; web 2023, patagoniaandina]. Season dates for La Ascensión were not stated, so re-check.
- Camping: Caburé is a car campsite with fire pits at the portal (paid? not stated). Crowd: low to moderate; it is the park's busiest portal. Confidence: medium.

**6. Cerro Negro (Parque Patagonia), day (short). Verdict: ADD**
- Trailhead: "Virgen del Camino" on RP43, 5 km west of La Ascensión. OSM has Capilla Virgen del Buen Viaje at -46.5995, -71.4833. Summit: Cerro Negro (s) at -46.608, -71.527 [OSM agrees with the direction, but the trail itself is not mapped].
- 6 km, medium, summit 575 m; ≈350 m ascent, 2 to 3 h [web 2023-06]. T2. Combine with item 5 (same day or an easy half-day). Free. Confidence: medium.

**7. La Meseta via Puesto Amarillo to Puesto El Rincón dome, one night. Verdict: MAYBE**
- From La Ascensión (-46.6072, -71.4075) to the Puesto Amarillo campsite (-46.6544, -71.4171) and the Puesto El Rincón dome (OSM -46.7032, -71.452). OSM foot-routing gives 13.3 km to El Rincón, close to the stated ≈12 km [web 2023-06]. The park's full "A la Meseta" route is 18.2 km, high difficulty, with a night out [web 2026-11].
- ≈25 to 26 km in two days, ≈700 to 900 m ascent [estimate]. T3. Hazards: navigation, no water sources (not verified).
- The dome is free shelter; walk-in camping is at El Rincón; registration is required at the portal; the park says no advance booking. Weather on the meseta is harsh and wind is strong. Confidence: low to medium.

**NO (near-misses and excluded)**
- Cañadón Río Oro (to the base of Cerro San Lorenzo): the approach is on private land (Lagos del Furioso area, between Lagos Posadas and Pueyrredón, about 25 min from Lago Posadas) and the trip is multi-day [web, Aire Libre, undated]. It breaks the private-land and multi-day limits.
- Lago Azara / Cerro San Lorenzo: 52 km, 3 to 5 days, high difficulty. It breaks the no-multi-day limit [web 2024-10].
- Meseta del Lago Buenos Aires (guided Civitatis trek): guided, and the plateau is outside the park and "very hard to access" [web 2021, La Nación; Civitatis]. Not wanted.
- Cerro de la Virgen (Los Antiguos): reviews mention a short scramble [web Wanderlog, undated].
- Cerro de los Indios (OSM -47.596, -71.718, on RP39 near Lago Posadas), Cerro Colorado and Cerro Lápiz (OSM -47.119, -71.733, on RP41): I found no trail information, and no confirmed access or landowner status. Treat them as roadside viewpoints only. Not graded.

## B. Notes
- **PN Perito Moreno season and registration:** open 1 Oct to 30 Apr, and dates can change with road conditions [web 2026-11, argentina.gob.ar]. Register at the Centro Operativo Clemente Onelli (09:00 to 19:00). Overnight areas are booked by email at reservasperitomoreno@bancodebosques.org or via trekkingperitomoreno.com [web 2026-11, argentina.gob.ar]; an older article gives peritomoreno@apn.gob.ar and WhatsApp +54 9 2962 409358, with bookings processed Mon to Fri 08:00 to 16:00 and one month ahead [web 2024-10, Río Negro].
- **PN Perito Moreno fees 2026-27:** foreigner camping ARS 25,000 per site, refugio ARS 70,000 per person and domo ARS 40,000 per person, plus ARS 5,000 per person per night booking fee. These are from a Meteored article of unknown date, so verify them with the park. The APN entry fee is NOT confirmed. Lago Burmeister has 3 campsites (one tent, up to 4 people, one night) and 3 motorhome spots for 2 consecutive nights [web 2026, argentina.gob.ar]. A tent trip needs a booked site.
- **Car sleeping in PN Perito Moreno:** only designated areas (camp or motorhome sites); I found no rule allowing roadside sleeping in the park. Parque Patagonia: Caburé (La Ascensión, vehicle access), Los Choiques (El Sauco, free), and paid sites at Cañadón Pinturas (not all fees are stated) [web 2025-10].
- **Roads:**
  - RP41 south of Lago Posadas to PN Perito Moreno: opened 2021, 4x4 only, up to ≈1,500 m, usually Nov to Apr [web 2021 and 2024-10]. Do not drive it in bad weather.
  - Bajo Caracoles to Lago Posadas (RP39): ≈72 to 74 km of gravel. A paving project was mentioned in an undated article [web, Vía País].
  - RN40 Perito Moreno to Bajo Caracoles: ≈124 km, gravel. RP43 Los Antiguos to La Ascensión: gravel, easy.
  - Paso Roballos: a ruta0.com listing shows "no cuts" but the report is over six months old, and another traveler report (also old) called the Bajo Caracoles to Roballos gravel poor. I found no 2026-27 road bulletin. Ask locals and check Vialidad Nacional Santa Cruz in January.
- **Fuel:** Chile Chico and Los Antiguos have fuel. I could not confirm fuel in Bajo Caracoles, Lago Posadas or Monte Zeballos. Assume Bajo Caracoles is limited and fill up at Perito Moreno or Los Antiguos. Carry a spare can for the RP41/RP37 side [estimate].
- **Best bases:** Los Antiguos (services, 17 km from La Ascensión; shops and fuel) for the Parque Patagonia hikes. Lago Posadas village for the Posadas/Pueyrredón side and RP41. For the park hikes, book Lago Burmeister or the La Oriental area (shop-free; bring all food).
- **Crowd level:** the PN Perito Moreno hikes are very quiet. La Ascensión is the busiest portal here but still quiet.
- **Border and driving times from Chile Chico:** Los Antiguos is about 10 min over the border. La Ascensión is about 45 to 60 min total, including border formalities [estimate]. Bajo Caracoles via Paso Roballos is about 3.5 h [estimate]. PN Perito Moreno is a long day.
- **Catalogue:** only `cuevamanos` (Cueva de las Manos, guided activity) exists for this area. Nothing here duplicates it. Cañadón Pinturas and Portal Cueva de las Manos (Parque Patagonia) are close to it, with 50 km of trails, some open and some guided, opened 2025-10 [web 2025-10].
