# GPT70–72, 76–77, 29H–31H: Futaleufú, Pumalín, Coyhaique

*Scouting report by a research subagent, 22 Nov 2026. Source: GPT Hikers Manual (Oct 2025) plus web sources listed below. Verdicts and figures are the agent's; verify before relying on them.*

## Northern Aysén / Palena (GPT70, 71, 72, 76, 77, 29H, 30H, 31H): scouting result

**Summary:** One of these GPT sections offers a worthwhile day hike, a lake-and-forest settler trail just north-west of Futaleufú (MAYBE). Nothing else qualifies. This part of the GPT is mostly road walking, ferry, packrafting, or long remote treks with hard fords. The manual's detail pages for all these sections are marked "(To be updated)" and are only maps, elevation profiles and summary tables, so my numbers come from those plus wikiexplora trip reports.

### Futaleufú: Lago Leal – Lago Las Rosas trail (start of GPT71)  — verdict: MAYBE
- **GPT ref:** GPT71 regular hiking route, km ≈ 0–17 (Futaleufú → Lago Leal → Lago Las Rosas → bridge over Río del Nordeste at km 16.9). Manual pp. 930–933: summary table, satellite map 102, elevation profile 111.
- **Car access point:** Futaleufú town (≈ -43.18, -71.87). The route leaves NW on a gravel road, then a dirt road. With the 4x4 you can probably drive the first ≈ 3–5 km to the foot of the climb. Exact road end unknown.
- **Hike:** out-and-back. From the base of the climb to the high point above Lago Las Rosas (km ≈ 15) and back is ≈ 20–22 km and ≈ 700–800 m ascent, ≈ 6–7 h. A shorter version turns at the first high point (≈ 660 m, km 7), about 8–10 km and ≈ 350 m. A Wikiloc track "Lago las rosas" is 6 km / +372 m, so a shorter approach exists, but I could not find where it starts.
- **Terrain:**
  - Clear path, with a steep climb above Lago Leal.
  - Lago Las Rosas trail "perfectly marked and daily used" (2019) and "maintained, surprisingly good shape" (Mar 2026). Several gates.
  - No fords in this stretch; the bad fords and the 1,000 m pass come much later (km 37–67).
  - Not exposed. Max ≈ 700 m altitude, no snow risk. Navigation easy.
- **Fees / private land / permits:**
  - At the north end of Lago Leal the GPT track crosses a settler's property (manual waypoint "Settler 11.2"). A marked trail further NW along the hillside avoids it (Mar 2026 report); use that.
  - Locals also run horse tours to Las Rosas, so the trail is in regular use. No fees reported.
- **Current status:**
  - wikiexplora GPT71, trip report of 6–7 Mar 2026: trail in good shape, bridge at km 16.9 intact.
  - Komoot viewpoint "View of Lago Las Rosas", rated 17 Feb 2025.
- **Why it's worth it:** It adds a lake / old-growth forest / settler-valley walk to Futaleufú, which Plan 5 currently covers only with Piedra del Águila and short Reserva Nacional Futaleufú trails.
- **Fits:** Plan 5, Jan 31: either as an alternative to the Reserva Nacional Futaleufú trails, or keep Piedra del Águila as a short morning walk and do Las Rosas as the main hike. The short version also fits the afternoon of Jan 30 if they arrive early. No extra driving.
- **Confidence:** medium-low. Uncertain: how far the car can go, the exact distance and ascent (estimated from the manual's profile), and where the "Lago Leal" trailhead is (the name doesn't appear in other sources; a 2011 blog calls the area Lago Noroeste). Ask the Futaleufú tourist office.

### GPT71 rest: Espolón valley and pass to El Amarillo  — verdict: NO
- **GPT ref:** GPT71 km ≈ 37–93 (pp. 930–933; Difficulty 4/5, listed challenges include "Demanding River Fords, Bush Bashing, Possibly Impassable").
- **Why not:**
  - The Río Espolón fords were mid-thigh and fast (2025 and Mar 2026 reports).
  - The 1,000 m pass has a very steep, eroded descent.
  - The northern part is mud, swamp and bamboo with hard navigation.
  - From the El Amarillo end, the first ≈ 25 km are road before the trail even starts.
- It can't be cut down to a day hike or an overnight within the limits.

### GPT76 Pumalín Sur coast (Chaitén – Chana – Loyola)  — verdict: NO
- **GPT ref:** GPT76 (pp. 946–949): Attraction 2/5, flat coast (max 120 m), 20 km of "partly muddy trails" north of Chana, "Demanding River Fords".
- **Why not:** Reports describe a stretch that depends on the tide and is "practically impassable" on foot, plus poor cattle trails. The Pumalín trails already in Plan 5 are far better.

### GPT77, GPT70, GPT72, GPT29H, GPT31H  — verdict: NO
- GPT77 is a ferry for hikers.
- GPT70 is 100% road.
- GPT72 is mostly roads along the Futaleufú river to Palena (Attraction 1/5).
- GPT29H is 100% primary road.
- GPT31H is gravel roads through farmland.

### GPT30H near Coyhaique / Mañihuales  — verdict: NO
- **Trail near Coyhaique:** The only trail stretch near Coyhaique is the end, which runs through or along Reserva Nacional Coyhaique, already in all plans.
- **The ≈ 1,250–1,500 m crossing (km 72–83):**
  - It is steep off-trail forest bush-bashing up to an exposed treeless ridge, with snow and strong wind reported in Feb 2025.
  - On the way there, near km 52, a landowner asked hikers to get permission to pass.
- **Rest of the section:**
  - Hikers were stopped by locals near km 107 (2026).
  - Variant 01 crosses a military area, where hikers were detained (Feb 2025).
  - The Río Ñireguao ford was chest-deep in Nov 2022.

**Things to know for the existing plans in this area:**

- **Pumalín (CONAF-run since 2019):**
  - Entry and trails are free and no guide or permit is needed.
  - Campgrounds (El Volcán, Grande, Ventisquero, Cascada Escondida, etc.) are reported as first-come, first-served and paid; I saw CLP 6,000 pp/night quoted for summer in a 2025 article. Unconfirmed for 2026.
  - Sources give the trail hours as about 08:30–17:30, with **last trail entry 14:00** (2025 article citing CONAF).
  - The Ventisquero Amarillo trail is ≈ 20 km and ≈ 500 m ascent, ≈ 6 h (the last ≈ 3 km boggy), so start in the morning. Doing it as an afternoon add-on won't work.
  - Termas El Amarillo were closed for construction in Jan 2025.
  - I found no 2025–26 official closure notice for the Volcán Chaitén trail; the most recent reports (2022–23) say open. Email loslagos.oirs@conaf.cl to confirm.
- **Queulat (Ventisquero Colgante):** Recent guides say tickets must be bought in advance on pasesparques.cl and spots are limited. Book before the Puyuhuapi days.
- **Reserva Nacional Coyhaique (pasesparques.cl):** Open **Tuesday–Sunday only**, 09:00–16:30, last entry 14:00. Online booking with no refunds. Fee for foreigners was CLP 6,000–8,500 in older reports.
  - None of the planned Coyhaique-reserve days falls on a Monday: Jan 28 (Wed), Feb 7 (Sat), Feb 19 (Thu), Feb 20 (Fri).
  - On afternoon-arrival days (e.g. Plan 1 Feb 19 and Plan 4 Feb 20, which have 95 km of driving before the loop), they must be at the gate by 14:00.
