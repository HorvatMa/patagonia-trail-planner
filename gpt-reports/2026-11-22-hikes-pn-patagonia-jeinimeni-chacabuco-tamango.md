## A. Candidates (best first)

**1. Lago Verde camp, Jeinimeni sector: one-night tent trip (MAYBE)**
- Trailhead and access: CONAF Jeinimeni / El Silencio camp area at Lago Jeinimeni. Take road X-753 from Chile Chico, about 60 km, gravel with river fords near the lake [web 2026-11 pasesparques]. A high-clearance 4x4 is needed, and the rivers must be low. Rental insurance usually excludes water damage.
- Route: the trail from El Silencio camp to Lago Verde passes Laguna Esmeralda and then climbs a steep rise. This path was renovated, with a new swinging bridge at the Lago Verde mouth in Dec 2023 [web, 2023 comment on Gaia]. Out-and-back to the camp at Lago Verde.
- Size: roughly 20–30 km return over 2 days, ≈300–450 m ascent, ≈4–6 h each way. [estimate] Sources disagree: Gaia gives 15.8 km one way from El Silencio, while the catalogue says "≈10 km return" for the Lago Verde walk. Ask the ranger.
- SAC and hazards: T2/T3, ford, navigation. The main ford is the Río Jeinimeni, about 0.8 km past Laguna Esmeralda. It is usually ankle to knee early season. After rain it reaches mid-thigh, which is ford-deep. Don't cross above the knee. [web adventurealan; wikiexplora 2026 reports ankle to knee, mid-thigh after rain]
- Fees and booking:
  - Jeinimeni is open Jan–Apr, daily 09:00–18:00, with the last entry at 18:00 [web 2026-11 pasesparques]. A pasesparques.cl ticket is shown. No amounts are listed and tickets are non-refundable.
  - Park entry is about 11,400 CLP per foreign adult [web 2025-08, sallysees]. Camping about 8,000–10,000 CLP per person [web, older]. Re-check, because these figures are not 2027 prices.
  - Reserva Jeinimeni requires designated campsites (no wild camping). Fire rules are unknown, so assume no fires and bring a stove [estimate].
- Current status: pasesparques showed no closures as of 2026-11. A 2026-season log reports a closure after heavy rain, plus a helicopter rescue at Valle Hermoso. Wikiexplora reports a Jan 2026 washout near -46.8897, -72.1647, which is on the Valle Hermoso side, not the Lago Verde route.
- Crowds Jan–Feb: low to moderate. The road access filters visitors, and the trail is used mostly by trekkers. [estimate]
- Why: a quiet lakeside tent camp with steppe-to-lenga scenery. The only 4x4 gravel is the Chile Chico–X-753 road.
- Confidence: low-medium. The distance, the exact camp and the trailhead are all unverified.

**2. Tamango: Laguna Cangrejo / Tamanguito area camp, one night (MAYBE)**
- Trailhead: Tamango entrance, 4 km from Cochrane town on a paved or gravel road. It is 2WD fine, and about 500 m from Camping Tamango. Opens 08:30, last entry 17:00 [web 2025-08].
- Route: trail towards the Tamango lakes, about 5.6 km each way (routed 5.6 km on OSM foot paths from the entrance to the camp point) and ≈400 m ascent [estimate]. Return the next day.
- SAC and hazards: T2. Some navigation on faint sections. The standard Paso Los Cóndores route crosses a gate on private property that may be locked, so I'd stay inside the reserve [web adventurealan]. Don't go beyond the reserve.
- Fees and booking:
  - Entry about 11,400 CLP per foreign adult [web 2025-08]. Camping Tamango (near the entrance, on Río Cochrane) is 8,000 CLP per person, with flush toilets and showers; booking by WhatsApp is probably unnecessary outside peak summer [web 2025-08].
  - Wild camping is reported allowed only at existing camps [web, older]. Ask the guardaparque. The coordinates come from hiker reports, not from CONAF.
- Crowds: low. The campsite was almost empty in April; Jan–Feb is busier but still quiet. [web 2025-08 plus estimate]
- Why: a short, easy tent trip, 20 min from town, with huemul possibility on the Los Huemules route (the catalogue has Tamango day trails).
- Confidence: medium for access and fees, low for the camp point.

**3. Upper Valle Aviles camp, one night (MAYBE)**
- Trailhead: Aviles trailhead at the catalogue's marker near West Winds (-47.1274, -72.5047), on a gravel road. Aviles has a suspension bridge, and camping is allowed only in upper Valle Aviles above it [web adventurealan, older].
- Route: out-and-back from the catalogue's Aviles trailhead up the valley to a camp above the bridge, with one night. ≈15–20 km return, ≈400–500 m [estimate]. The loop in the catalogue is 16 km.
- SAC and hazards: T2/T3 and ford. Río Aviles crossings are reported ankle to knee. One crossing path was reported destroyed by the river in 2025 [web wikiexplora]. The suspension bridge is high, so check it for exposure on site.
- Fees: Parque Patagonia entry, plus camping at 8,000 CLP per person if you use West Winds [web, older].
- Crowds: low to moderate.
- Why: this is the only wild-camp option in the main park, in a quiet valley.
- Confidence: low. The camp point is unknown, so no `camps` entry is given.

### Near-misses (NO)
- **Valle Hermoso overnight from Jeinimeni:** 15.6 km one way, ≈425 m ascent, 4.5 h [web adventurealan]. It has many fords, up to mid-thigh and hip-deep after rain (reports Dec 2025–Jan 2026), so it breaks ford-deep, and it is about 31 km return.
- **Paso Los Cóndores overnight from Cochrane to Lago Tamanguito:** ≈11 km each way, 915 m ascent. The main route goes through a private-land gate, which breaks the private-land rule.

## B. Notes
- **Road access:** Paso Roballos road (Cochrane–Paso Roballos–Los Antiguos) has no 2026 condition report. Ruta0 showed no cuts in 2026, with "con precaución" on one stretch and pickups recommended [web ruta0, undated]. A 4x4 is sensible. Confirm the border schedule and Vialidad status in January. X-753 from Chile Chico needs 4x4 and low rivers.
- **Fees:** all fees are old and must be re-checked. The pasesparques.cl Jeinimeni page lists no prices or fire rules.
- **Fords:** rivers rise after rain, and Jan 2026 saw cold, high water. Plan to wait or turn back if a ford is above the knee.
- **Sleeping in the car:** CONAF rules are unclear, so use Camping Tamango, Casa de Piedra (8,000–10,000 CLP per person, showers) or West Winds. Chile Chico and Los Antiguos have campgrounds.
- **Fuel:** Chile Chico and Cochrane have stations. Fill up before Roballos and X-753.
- **Catalogue:** banderas, piedra, jeinimeni (includes Laguna Esmeralda, Costanera), aviles, lagunas_altas and tamango already exist. I added no new day hikes. Chacabuco has others (Cañadón Chacabuco, Confluencia Baker–Chacabuco, Mirador Tompkins), but I couldn't find lengths. Ask CONAF or West Winds staff.
- **OSM check:** Nominatim agrees only for Paso Roballos (-47.1577, -71.8544) and Cerro Tamango (-47.1659, -72.5781). It has no entry for Lago Verde, Tamanguito, Elefantes or Esmeralda. OSM foot routing from Tamango to the camp gave 5.6 km (a path along the way). The catalogue's jeinimeni marker is 1.4 km from the Lago Verde camp but routes only to 924 m (59 snap points off), so treat it as approximate.
