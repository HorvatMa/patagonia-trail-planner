# GPT32–33H: Cerro Castillo and Puerto Ibáñez

*Scouting report by a research subagent, 22 Nov 2026. Source: GPT Hikers Manual (Oct 2025) plus web sources listed below. Verdicts and figures are the agent's; verify before relying on them.*

### Estero Parada → Campamento Porteadores → Campamento Neozelandés (+ optional Laguna Duff), from Villa Cerro Castillo — verdict: MAYBE (leaning ADD once the guide rule is confirmed)
- GPT ref: GPT32 regular route, last ≈8 km of the section (descent from Porteadores down Estero Parada to Villa Cerro Castillo, ≈km 58–67), plus the thin-line GPT32 option up the Neozelandés side valley. Manual pp. 743 (map) and 745 (elevation profile). The GPT32 detail pages have no written description.
- Car access: Estero Parada park entrance, a few km west of the village off the Carretera Austral or a local road (my estimate from the map is ≈4–5 km W/NW of the village; I did not find coordinates). Normal car; no 4x4 needed as far as I can tell.
- Hike: out-and-back, or a small loop via Porteadores. ≈20–22 km and ≈870–1,000 m ascent, 7–8 h. The concessionaire says "22 km day option"; a Komoot loop gives 20.7 km / 870 m with a high point of 1,150 m. Laguna Duff adds ≈1 h each way and a few hundred metres of climb, which pushes the day to the hard limits. Treat it as optional.
- Terrain: a good, marked park trail through lenga forest and stream crossings (bridged or small). It ends in a cirque at Neozelandés (≈1,200 m) under the north face of Cerro Castillo. No exposure or scrambling up to Neozelandés. I could not verify the last stretch to Laguna Duff. Snow is unlikely in Jan–Feb.
- Fees / permits: covered by the normal park day ticket, ≈20,000–21,250 CLP per foreigner (details in the closing paragraph). Entry 08:00–11:00 (until 15:00 if camping). Chile.travel (2021) says this trail is "guide only". The current concessionaire site (senderospncc.cl) lists it as a regular "day option with less technical demand" and mentions guides only for some seasonal restrictions; the guide-only rule may be a winter rule. **Must be confirmed at the park office in Villa Cerro Castillo, or on senderospncc.cl before buying.**
- Current status (web): senderospncc.cl (undated, current site) lists the trail with 08:00–11:00 entry. A Komoot tour was updated Nov 2026. A Wikiexplora trip report from the 2025/26 season notes that CONAF sends trekkers down via Estero Parada or the Laguna trail when conditions are bad, so the trail is open and in use.
- Why it's worth it: it shows a different side of Cerro Castillo (north face, glaciers, Laguna Duff) from the Laguna Cerro Castillo hike, which every plan already includes. It is a real second hike in the same village, on the GPT itself, with no extra driving.
- Fits: the Cerro Castillo flex day in every plan (Plan 1 Feb 2 or Feb 18; Plan 2 Jan 31 or Feb 15; Plan 3 Feb 16; Plan 4 Feb 19; Plan 5 Feb 9 or Feb 19). It also works as the "second chance" day if the Laguna hike is clouded out: the valley is lower and more sheltered, but the cirque view still needs a decent ceiling. Extra driving ≈10 min.
- Confidence: medium. Uncertain: whether a guide is required, the exact location of the entrance, and the exact distance and ascent to Neozelandés and Laguna Duff.

### Lago General Carrera shore trail from the Levicán side — verdict: MAYBE (low)
- GPT ref: GPT33H regular route ≈km 114–119 (out-and-back from where the minor road meets the lake shore at km 118.6), manual pp. 765–766.
- Car access: from Puerto Ibáñez take the Levicán gravel road (≈23 km), then an unsigned ranch track (Fundo Santa Luisa) for ≈8 km. Wikiexplora says only the first couple of km of that track are passable without high clearance or 4x4. The manual says this road was extended to the lake shore in 2019. From Villa Cerro Castillo it is ≈1.5–2 h each way.
- Hike: out-and-back, 8–14 km, ≈400–700 m ascent, because of constant ups and downs.
- Terrain: a horse trail with steep, rocky ups and downs, thorny scrub and a stream ford. If the lake is high you may have to wade ≈10 m beside a rock wall. The manual warns that parts of this coast trail run "on the edge of steep cliffs… tripping can be fatal". Those parts are mainly further west (≈km 104–115), so the couple would have to turn back early.
- Fees / private land: no fee. It crosses private ranch land: ask permission at the fundo and close gates.
- Current status: Wikiexplora page undated (2018 note). Nothing recent found.
- Why it's worth it: wild, empty lakeshore views. But the plans already show them the same lake from the ferry and from Chile Chico, and the cliff warning conflicts with their safety rule.
- Fits: only as a flex-day option in plans that end with the Chile Chico–Puerto Ibáñez ferry. Long driving on rough roads.
- Confidence: low (car access to the shore, how far the cliffy part starts, ford conditions).

### Torres de Avellano (pass viewpoint at 1,310 m) — verdict: NO
- GPT ref: GPT33H regular route km 45.7 (Avellano trailhead) → km 62.2 (pass), manual pp. 753, 762–764.
- Car access: Carretera Austral ≈20 km south of the village, then route X-727 (Lago Lapparent). Past the last house a 4x4 forestry track crosses two fords (Estero El Alto and Río Candongas) and a repaired washout. It ends at a **locked gate ≈2 h walk before the trailhead** (Wikiexplora).
- Hike: from the gate to the viewpoint is ≈23 km one way, so ≈46 km round trip (Wikiexplora's own total is 46.2 km). Even as a one-nighter it is too long.
- Terrain: the trail after the herders' huts is poorly visible with many fallen trees (manual km 54.6–57.1). Near the pass it crosses mallín wetland, moraine and a field of large boulders. The pass is weather-exposed. On the far side, the descent through boulders and bush is clearly beyond their limits.
- Fees: free, but it crosses private land. Ask permission at the first house ("Don Rolo").
- Why not: spectacular, but fails the distance limit, and the car access is a gamble with rental-insurance-unfriendly fords. The nearby Laguna La Plaza (≈2.6 km round trip from the trailhead, but ≈3–3.5 h from the gate) is not worth the drive either.
- Confidence: high.

### Laguna Cerro Castillo → Porteadores → Estero Parada loop — verdict: NO
Linking the existing Laguna hike with the GPT descent to Porteadores makes ≈22–25 km with ≈1,450 m of ascent, which is over the limit. It includes the steep loose-scree descent off the ridge. Entering by one trail and leaving by another also requires a 2-day ticket.

### GPT32 northern part and Option 1 (Villa Frei / Lago Elizalde → Las Horquetas) — verdict: NO
The manual rates it Difficulty 5/5, with scrambling over landslide terrain at ≈km 28 and alpine high points of 1,400–1,680 m. The start area is near Coyhaique, far from Villa Cerro Castillo. The Las Horquetas entrance is for one-way trekkers (they must arrive by 13:00) and does not suit an out-and-back day hike.

### GPT33H Option 1 (Valle Ibáñez) — verdict: NO
The manual (p. 769) says it is mostly minor roads, apart from a northern third on trail (an old Sendero de Chile fragment), and that it is "easy and scenic". Nothing on it beats the planned drive-by visit to the Salto del Río Ibáñez and Puerto Ibáñez.

**Other things to know.**

Brief vs. current dates: the brief says Jan–Feb 2026, but today is Nov 2026 and some sources already quote the 2026/27 season. The plans may refer to Jan–Feb 2027; please check.

Laguna Cerro Castillo hike, current rules:
- **Fees:** sources disagree.
  - The concessionaire, senderospncc.cl (current site, no year stated): 1-day pass 21,250 CLP for foreigners (17,750 Chilean). 2-day 29,750; 3-day 37,750.
  - Chile.travel (undated) and Andeshandbook (listed for 2026): 20,000 foreigner / 16,650 Chilean.
  - Sallysees (Sep 2025): 20,000.
  - Patagonia Scout (Jul 2026) quotes 30,000 CLP for 2026/27, but that refers to the trek. It is unconfirmed and conflicts with the others.
- **Landowner fee:** the separate landowner fee the manual mentions (5,000 CLP in 2020) no longer seems to be charged separately. It now appears to be one ticket sold through the concession. A private trailhead also still exists.
- **Payment:** budget ≈21,000 CLP per person, card only at the ranger station, or buy online at pasesparques.cl or senderospncc.cl. One concessionaire day-pass listing showed "sold out", so online pre-purchase in peak season is prudent. I found no confirmation of a reported "40 hikers per day" quota.
- **Start time:** you must start between 08:00 and 12:00. Several reports say you will be turned away after that, so the plans' Laguna day should start early.
- **Registration:** register with the online waiver via QR code at the ranger station; tickets are checked at a ≈5 km control point.
- **Trailhead:** official trailhead on dirt road X-720, ≈5 km from the village. The last ≈1.6 km is signed 4x4-only.
- **Weather closures:** the final ≈1.5 km to the lagoon can close at short notice in bad weather.
- **Season:** the 2025/26 season opened 21 Nov 2025.
- **Trek closure:** Campamento El Bosque is currently closed to camping. This affects only trekkers.

Lago General Carrera: the ferry and lake crossings are often disrupted by wind (manual), so keep the flex day after the ferry as a true buffer.
