## B. Notes for the existing plans
- **Drives:**
  - Lago Verde is about 70 km one way from La Junta on X-10, mostly gravel. Allow 1.5 to 2 h each way [estimate]. It is a dead end, so do not plan a loop to La Tapera.
  - La Tapera is on the X-25 side road from the Carretera Austral near Villa Amengual. A plan that drives Lago Verde then La Tapera must go via La Junta and Ruta 7, about 327 km [web, undated].
  - Raúl Marín Balmaceda is another 70 km of gravel on the X-12 from La Junta [from the earlier report].
- **Fuel and services:** fuel at La Junta, Puyuhuapi and Puerto Cisnes. Carry cash. Lago Verde and Villa Amengual have small shops. La Tapera has several shops that often close around 13:00 to 17:00 [web 2024-12].
- **Bus days:** Lago Verde from La Junta on Wednesdays, La Tapera to Coyhaique on Wed/Fri/Sun [web 2024-26 logs]. This only matters if the car breaks down.
- **Border:** Paso Las Pampas to Argentina is 5 km from Lago Verde and open only part of the year [web interpatagonia, undated]. Do not plan a crossing without checking.
