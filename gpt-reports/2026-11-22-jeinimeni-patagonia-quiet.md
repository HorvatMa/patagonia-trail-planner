### Jeinimeni portal to Valle Hermoso refugio (km 53 to 69), one night, turn back at the refugio — verdict: ADD* (confirm river level with the ranger on the day)
- GPT ref: GPT35 regular route km 53 to about 69. Manual pp. 417-418 (commentary), 783-786 (profile, "to be updated") [manual p.418].
- Car access: Portal Jeinimeni / El Silencio camp, about -46.8502, -72.1061 (taken from the earlier hike-researcher report; the OSM check below could not confirm it). Road X-753 from Chile Chico, about 60 km of rough gravel with fords near the lake. High-clearance 4x4. Check the X-753 ford depths with CONAF or in Chile Chico the day before.
- Hike: out-and-back, about 16 km each way, about 32 km in total over 2 days. Ascent about 300-450 m each way, so about 6 h per day [estimate, from reports 03 and the researcher]. Destination: Valle Hermoso refugio and camp, about -46.905, -72.165 [estimate only]. Km 68.1-69 is where the camp is; the exact point comes from hikers' notes, not a source I could pin down.
- Terrain: marked trail, T2/T3. Hazard tags: ford, navigation.
  - Fords up to km 69: the km 63.4 ford is usually dry, and the fords at km 66.1, 66.4 and 67.0 are at most knee-deep [web 2023-02, 2024-02, 2025-01 wikiexplora: Ondrej & Bara, Lilian & Rainbow, Sean & Neele (knee-deep on a rainy evening), MiaimZelt (knee at km 67.0)]. Fords between km 68.4 and 71.6 are many and below the knee (Feb 2024, Jan 2025).
  - Beyond km 69 the fords get harder. They are mid-thigh in several reports, and one at km 71.2 was "mighty" [web 2024-02 Tomas & Natalie]. Do not go past km 69.
  - Latest reports, 2025-11 to 2026-02:
    - 2025-11 (Ohad): never above the knee.
    - 2025-12 (Emily & Kole, Bailey, Massa & Jacicek): easy to mid-thigh.
    - 2026-01 (Dave & Siyuan): over 20 fords, all below the knee.
    - 2026-01 (Mor, Tomas M.): at most mid-thigh at km 69-72, which is beyond the turnaround.
    - 2026-01 (Chris & Lotte): about hip-high during snowfall.
    - 2026-02 (Denis & Robert): knee-deep at most, 20 fords between km 66.9 and 72.4.
    - 2026-02 (Sander & Isa): never above mid-thigh for a 165 cm hiker.
  - Conclusion: at normal and low water the turnaround at km 69 is within the knee limit, but the margin is slim. The researcher's "waist-deep after rain" is true after heavy rain only (2025-02: Pierre-Marie found rivers in flood; 2023-03: park closed and a helicopter rescue at Valle Hermoso). The river rose about 15 cm and went brown after one day of rain on 2025-02-09. Rule: do not cross if the water is brown or the previous day was rainy; wait a day (you have flex days).
  - Washout: the trail was swept away at -46.8897, -72.1647 (2026-01-12 Chris & Lotte; 2026-01-02 Mor). Use the OSM detour and fords marked on the GPT track. Fallen trees before and after the pass near km 74.9 are beyond the turnaround.
- Fees / private land / permits: park entry about CLP 11,400 per foreigner per person, paid online at pasesparques.cl [web 2025-01 MiaimZelt, 2026-02 Sander]. Reports differ on camping: free at El Silencio (2025-02, 2024-03), CLP 6,000 extra in 2023, and CLP 17,000 in 2024-03 that covered the park campgrounds. Valle Hermoso refugio has rain-sheltered tables [web 2026-02 Sander]. Designated sites only. No private land.
- Current status: no closure report for the season. Hikers walked it until late Feb 2026. CONAF/pasesparques shows "Jan-Apr 09:00-18:00" (per the researcher report, Nov 2026). Park sectors close in early April [web 2023-04].
- Why it's worth it: Valle Hermoso (glacier-hung valley) is the real scenery of the Aviles traverse. Unlike the Lago Verde walk, it needs a night out. It has few visitors apart from trekkers (one group had four other groups at the camp in Feb 2025).
- Fits: any plan with a Chile Chico stay. Suggest the first of the 0-2 overnight slots in a Chile Chico / Lago General Carrera plan. It would replace the Jeinimeni lakeshore day and the next flex day (two days, one night, with a spare flex day for rain). Day index: not assigned, since the plans file doesn't exist.
- Confidence: medium. Uncertain: river levels on the day, the camp coordinates, whether a camping fee applies.

### Short variant: Raleigh camp (km 61.7) or Lago Verde east-shore shelter, one night — verdict: MAYBE
- GPT ref: GPT35 km 53 to 61.7, or on to Lago Verde. Option D ends at a shelter on Lago Verde's east shore, which was rated nicer than the Valle Hermoso refugio (2023-02 Veronica & Zach).
- Car access: same as above.
- Hike: about 9 km each way, about 18 km in total [estimate]. Trail, T2. The only fords before km 63.4 are small; there is a bridge at km 58.9 and a new hanging bridge by Raleigh over the Rio San Antonio (2023-12, 2024-03). The shore walk km 56.65-57.75 was highly recommended (2024-02).
- Camp: Raleigh, with picnic tables and a cooking shelter [web 2023-02, 2026-01 Chris & Lotte]. A ranger warned about black worms in the forest, so camp by a bridge in the open and not under trees (2025-01).
- Fees: park entry about CLP 11,400 per person; camping rules as above.
- Why: a low-risk fallback if the rivers are up. It is only a modest step beyond the Jeinimeni day hikes already in the plans.
- Confidence: medium-low (camp coordinates not available).

### Meseta lagoons from X-753 (km 41 to 30.6) — verdict: already ADD in report 03, additional notes
- Plateau lagoon hikers' notes 2023-12 to 2026-02: water was plentiful on the plateau (2025-12 Massa; 2025-02 Michael & Kasia). One report saw still, slow water with dead guanaco and goose droppings and advised filtering (2025-11 Ohad). Flamingos were seen in the central plateau in 2026-01 (Tomas M.) but not in 2026-02 (Sander). The plateau is "crazy windy" (2024-11 Jonas; 2023-03 Martin & Helena; 2023-02/03 Anna & Christopher). Water and grass sites at km 23.9 and 30.6.
- Gate: a closed iron gate at the settler's property had to be opened (2025-01 Al & Bee); another report had a fence door after the road (2024-11 Jonas); gates were easy in 2023-12. The land is settler land, so ask for permission before going through the gate; it conflicts with the "avoid private land" preference if the owner objects.
- I did not add this as a new candidate, only these notes.

- GPT35 Option 4 (Paso Roballos) is a remote exploration route [manual p.418, line 9705] with no worthwhile out-and-back day hike from a car point, so no candidate.
- Pass at -47.1577, -71.8544; OSM Nominatim agrees (Paso Roballos, Cochrane).

Reports are rated "con precaución", and one says "only pickups", with only 4x4 crossing from the village [web ruta0, undated, probably older than 6 months]. Border hours: Argentina's page lists 08:00-20:00; ruta0 says 08:00-22:00 from December to April [web ruta0, argentina.gob.ar, accessed 2026-11]. The two sources differ. Check with the Argentine authorities in January.
- On the Argentine side (Los Antiguos to Perito Moreno National Park), the Parque Patagonia Argentina trails (La Ascensión, 20 km from Los Antiguos; Cañadón Pinturas on RN 40; El Sauco, with free camping) need trekking registration, and I could not find 2026 conditions or fees [web 2026-08, argentina.gob.ar].
