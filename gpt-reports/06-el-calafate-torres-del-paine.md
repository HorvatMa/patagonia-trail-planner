# GPT41–46: El Calafate and Torres del Paine (Plan 4)

*Scouting report by a research subagent, 22 Nov 2026. Source: GPT Hikers Manual (Oct 2025) plus web sources listed below. Verdicts and figures are the agent's; verify before relying on them.*

## GPT41–46 scouting for Plan 4 (El Calafate / Torres del Paine)

The manual pages for GPT41–46 are marked "To be updated". They contain only summary tables, satellite maps and elevation profiles, so the route detail below comes from those maps and profiles plus wikiexplora trip reports from 2023–25. The result is thin: one stretch is worth adding, and the rest fail on permits, guide rules, distance or terrain.

### Las Carretas trail (Administración → towards Paine Grande), optionally with Mirador Ferrier — verdict: ADD
- **GPT ref:** GPT45 regular route, roughly km 169–191 (Paine Grande → Administración → Río Serrano CONAF, the section end). The manual's profile on p. 867 shows this stretch as flat, 100 m down to 30 m. Wikiexplora trip logs place Paine Grande at km 169.0 and the Serrano ranger station at km 191.6, which matches the 17.5 km length of Las Carretas. Mirador Ferrier is not on the GPT; it starts from the same car park and is added as the climb.
- **Car access:** CONAF Administración (Lago Toro sector), about -51.18, -72.96 (approximate). The park gravel road is fine for any car, so 4x4 is not needed. It is about 20 km from the Lago Grey area and about 45–50 km from Laguna Amarga.
- **Hike:** out-and-back.
  - Las Carretas towards the Las Carretas campsite (km 10–11) and back: about 20 km, about 200 m ascent, 5–6 h.
  - Combo, better: Ferrier (about 6.2 km round trip, about 590 m, about 3 h; Komoot) plus Las Carretas 5–6 km each way. That totals about 17–18 km and about 800 m.
- **Terrain:** Las Carretas is an official, mostly flat trail through open grassland and lagoons towards Lago Pehoé and the Paine Grande / Cuernos massif. It is often very windy, with no fords, no exposure and easy navigation. Ferrier is a steep, well-trodden path to a windy top at about 600 m with no scrambling. Snow risk in Jan–Feb is minimal.
- **Fees / permits:** covered by the normal park entry ticket. No refugio or campsite booking is needed for a day hike.
  - Latest entry times (CONAF 2025–26 bulletin): Las Carretas 14:00 from Administración, Ferrier 13:00 (closes 17:00). So do Ferrier first, in the morning.
  - Car sleeping: the same bulletin lists **free designated overnight spots for motorhomes at the CONAF Administración and the Pingo ranger station**, with 24/7 toilets and no showers. Sleeping in a car is very likely fine there; confirm with the ranger. This saves a campsite fee.
- **Current status (web):** both trails are listed as open, with the times above, in the CONAF 2025–26 bulletin (Oct 2025). Las Carretas length is 17.5 km per Wikivoyage (Sep 2025) and torresdelpaine.com. Ferrier figures are from Komoot (user track, undated).
- **Why it's worth it:** it is the only quiet, non-W stretch of GPT in the park that a day hiker can walk independently. It gives the full frontal panorama of the Paine massif across the lagoons, and Ferrier adds a bird's-eye view over Lago del Toro, Pehoé and the massif. It costs nothing extra.
- **Fits:** Plan 4, **Feb 9 (replace the TdP flex day)**. Sleep at Administración on the night of Feb 8 after the Grey day (Grey is only about 20 km away), hike on Feb 9, and sleep there again. On Feb 10 the drive back to Chaltén is about 1 h longer than from Laguna Amarga.
- **Confidence: medium-high.** Uncertain: the 2026–27 bulletin (not yet seen) could change the overnight-spot list or entry times.

### GPT44 regular route: junction X44 on RP15 (near Lago Roca) → border Hito III-40 — verdict: NO
- **GPT ref:** GPT44 regular route, second half (manual pp. 862–864). The profile climbs from 200 m to about 710 m around km 25–43.
- **Car access:** RP15 gravel road towards Lago Roca, roughly 40–50 km from El Calafate.
- **Hike:** out-and-back to the border, probably 35–40 km. A hiker in Dec 2023 needed a full day to walk it one way.
- **Terrain:** cross-country at first, then an old jeep track fading into cow trails. Boggy meadows. The main river ford was thigh-deep in Dec 2023, and the hiker went 3 km upstream to cross.
- **Fees / permits:** crosses private farmland. The last about 6 km are inside PN Los Glaciares; a local guide told the hiker a permit was required and threatened to report him to the rangers.
- **Current status:** wikiexplora reports from Dec 2023.
- **Why rejected:** too long, ford above the knee-height limit, unclear permit, and the manual rates its attraction only 2 of 5.
- **Confidence:** medium.

### GPT42: Estancia Helsingfors → Glaciar Upsala / Estancia Cristina — verdict: NO
- **GPT ref:** GPT42, pp. 856–858.
- **Car access:** Helsingfors, about 70 km of gravel each way off the Chaltén–Calafate road.
- **Why rejected:**
  - The manual rates it difficulty 5 of 5: alpine terrain, glaciers, demanding fords, bush-bashing, and 64% of the route still "investigation".
  - PN Los Glaciares told a hiker in Dec 2023 that the Puerto Bandera / Cristina–Helsingfors area needs **at least 3 people and prior email permission**. A couple doesn't qualify.
  - Helsingfors is a private luxury estancia.
  - The only walkable part, about 10 km along the Lago Viedma shore, doesn't justify about 3 h of gravel driving each way.
- **GPT41** is road walking and **GPT43** is a ferry crossing, so neither has anything to offer.

### GPT45 Option 8: Pingo–Zapata valley (Glaciar Zapata / Tyndall views) — verdict: NO
- **GPT ref:** GPT45 Option 8 (manual p. 425; the text calls it "Mirador Zapala").
- **Car access:** Pingo ranger station (Guardería Lago Grey).
- **Why rejected:**
  - The CONAF 2025–26 bulletin lists **Pingo–Zapata as closed** and Pingo–Chorrillos as **certified guide only**.
  - A June 2026 park document adds a cattle-control closure from 20 Apr to 10 Oct 2026. No reopening notice found.
  - Wikiexplora notes that a guide or a CONAF expedition permit is needed for Valle Zapata.

### Other GPT45 / GPT46 parts — verdict: NO
- **GPT45 "Aonikenk" area (Portería Sarmiento–Laguna Amarga, petroglyphs, about 7 km one way):** the CONAF 2025–26 bulletin lists the Aonikenk trail as certified guide only. This looks like the route the manual suggests south of Lago Nordenskjöld, but that is a likely match, not confirmed.
- **GPT45 north of Laguna Azul to the border:** about 44 km of gravel road walking plus a valley with fords that were thigh-deep (Jan 2024).
- **GPT45 Option 1 bypass (east of Lago Sarmiento):** roads only.
- **GPT46 Cerro Tenerife:** the manual rates it difficulty 5 of 5, with a 1,238 m exposed pass. The rest of the section is road and fjord walking near Puerto Natales, which is not on Plan 4's route anyway.
- **GPT44 variant 44-03 (El Calafate → Cerro Castillo, Chile):** a 5-day steppe crossing (Apr 2025 report) over estancia land, with Río Centinela fords just above knee height. It is not a day hike.

### Things to know for Plan 4's existing days
- **Base Las Torres (Feb 7):**
  - Under the 2025–26 rules, the latest entry from the Laguna Amarga ranger station is **10:00 in Dec–Feb**, and hikers must leave the lookout by 16:00.
  - No booking beyond the park ticket was needed. CONAF said there would be no Base Torres access regulation in 2026, and hiking without a guide has been allowed since 1 Sep 2025.
  - A shuttle runs Laguna Amarga → Welcome Center/Central for CLP 4,500 each way, cash only.
- **Park tickets are sold only online, in advance, at pasesparques.cl.** There is no internet at the park entrances, so screenshot the ticket.
  - A new fee system started 1 May 2026, with a daily ticket and a 2–10-day ticket. One source (redimin.cl, 2026) lists foreign adults at CLP 25,000 per day or **CLP 50,000 for 2–10 days**. December 2025 news cited about CLP 80,000. Check before buying.
  - For Plan 4's four TdP days, buy one multi-day ticket each.
- **Trip dates:** the brief says Jan–Feb 2026, but today is 22 Nov 2026. If the trip is really Jan–Feb 2027, the new fee rules apply, and the 2026–27 CONAF bulletin should be checked once it is published.
- **Perito Moreno (Feb 5):** PN Los Glaciares charges foreigners ARS 50,000 (rates from 1 June 2026). A 3-day Flexipass is ARS 100,000 and is also valid at the El Chaltén gate, which now charges too.
- **The Grey item on Feb 8:** "Mirador Glaciar Grey" in the bulletin is the lookout beyond Refugio Grey on the W trek, which can't be reached as a day hike from the road. The plan presumably means the Grey beach / peninsula walk from the Lago Grey car park (latest entry 16:00), which is fine.
- **Free or cheap sleeping:**
  - Inside the park: the free CONAF spots at Administración and the Pingo ranger station (above). Paid options are Camping Pehoé and Río Serrano.
  - Outside or near Laguna Azul: reports say the campsite costs about CLP 12,000 and needs no park ticket (Jan 2024).
  - Wild camping inside the park is fined.
- **Rules:** off-trail walking is banned and trails close in strong wind.
- **Wind:** the manual warns GPT45–50 are among the windiest sections.
