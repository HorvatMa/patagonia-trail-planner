# GPT scouting reports (22 Nov 2026)

Six research subagents each read one part of the *Greater Patagonian Trail Hikers Manual* (Jan Dudeck, Oct 2025, in the parent folder) and checked recent web sources. Their job was to find GPT stretches that fit the trip's rules:

- day hikes up to about 25 km and 1,300 m of climb, starting and ending at the car;
- no scrambling, exposed passes, glaciers or Tyrolean traverses, and no fords above the knee;
- easy open terrain without a trail is acceptable;
- one-night trips are listed separately as optional.

The reports below are the agents' own words, unedited. Their figures and claims come from the manual and online sources dated in each report, so verify anything important on the spot.
