### Refugio Toni Röhrer / Campo De Agostini, one night — verdict: MAYBE (status unconfirmed; low priority)
- GPT ref: not in the manual text. The only source is wikiexplora's GPT36H page, which lists the upper Río Tranquilo valley under GPT36H explorations [web 2026-11].
- Car access: Fundo San Lorenzo (Soto family), via roads X-901 then X-903 from Cochrane. The fundo coordinates are not found. OSM places the hut at -47.5181, -72.3158 (Nominatim "Refugio Toni Rohrer"), and the hut is the destination. The Calluqueo road (see the earlier report) shares the first part of the route.
  - The last stretch is a poor track and probably needs a 4x4 [web, older blogs, undated].
  - A 2.5 h drive from Cochrane was reported in March 2025 [web 2025-03, wikiexplora, Thijmen & Volker].
  - Drivers should ask about the river ford: older blogs mention one, but I found no recent source on whether cars must cross it. That affects rental-insurance cover.
  - I could not tell whether the 2016 blog's 9.5 km hut walk or the 2025 report's ≈2 h walk is right.
- Hike: one night, out-and-back. Estimated 2 × 9.5 km and about +325 m each way [estimate, 2016 report]. Day 2 would be an optional short walk up the valley, staying below Paso del Comedor.
- Terrain: trail with a stream crossing in a canyon. Tag: ford, private-land. The route above the hut is out of bounds: Paso del Comedor (≈2,000 m) was heavy snow with difficult navigation in March 2025, and glacier travel is reported on the Calluqueo side.
- Fees: the hut costs ≈7,000 CLP per person [web 2025-03]. Camping was reported at 10,000 CLP per person (undated iOverlander, probably older). Andeshandbook gave 3,000 CLP in 2015, so the figures are stale. It is private land with an operator, so ask permission and use their site.
- Current status: the hut had operators in March 2025 [web 2025-03]. A hut-use note appears under "Season 2026/27" on wikiexplora, but it is from the far side of the pass and gives no details [web 2026-11]. Searches for 2025–26 camping or road information returned nothing [web 2026-11].
- Why: the Chilean side of Monte San Lorenzo with almost no visitors. Against that, it is 5 h of gravel driving for a 19 km walk, and the quiet Calluqueo area already gives similar scenery.
- Fits: not assigned, because the plans are not written. If used, it needs Cochrane as a base and an overnight slot. Do not count it as one of the 2 optional overnights until the road and fee are confirmed.
- Confidence: low.

- Fits: any Cochrane or Calluqueo day, as a stop on the way. It replaces nothing.
- Confidence: medium on location, low on any trail details.

### Mirador Laguna Calluqueo (drive-up only, no glacier trail) — verdict: ADD* (confirm the road gate)
- GPT ref: GPT36H km 50.0, commentary p. 418. This repeats the earlier report, but with new numbers.
- Car access: viewpoint at -47.5972, -72.4972 (Lilian, April 2024, wikiexplora). OSM places Laguna Calluqueo at -47.5938, -72.4744. That is about 2 km east of the viewpoint and is a point on the lake, so the two are consistent. The road is good gravel, and non-4x4 cars have been reported OK [web 2024-02, Tomáš, wikiexplora]. A camp near the glacier is at -47.5938, -72.4996 [web 2024-04].
- Hike: for the walk to the lake edge and viewpoint, expect 1–3 km [estimate]. Skip the trail to the glacier front, which has the Río Pedregoso ford and the exposed traverse flagged in the earlier report.
- Terrain: T1 for the viewpoint. Tags: private-land (a gate beyond the mirador).
- Fees: none reported.
- Why: a free view of Monte San Lorenzo and the Calluqueo glacier. It is a half-day drive with a short walk. It combines with a night of wild car camping near the viewpoint.
- Fits: not assigned. It makes sense as a Cochrane-area day or half day.
- Confidence: high on the drive-up view, medium on the road gate.

## B. Notes for the existing plans
- The Calluqueo road is reported by several hikers as good gravel suitable for non-4x4 cars (Feb 2024, Feb 2025). One report mentions about 30 km of it near the start as paved [web 2025-02, wikiexplora, Graham & Gabe]. Fuel and time estimates should be rechecked against that.
- The Cochrane–Lago Brown minibus runs Mondays and Thursdays [web 2026-11, wikiexplora]. This is irrelevant to a car trip, but it shows how little traffic these roads get. Plan on being self-reliant for tyres and fuel.
- Camping Calafate in Cochrane charges 6,000 CLP per person, with a kitchen, showers and wifi [web 2026-11, wikiexplora, undated report].
- Villa O'Higgins has no ATM [manual p. 806, from the earlier report]. Bring cash.
- Check the opening days of CONAF Tamango and the Mosco glacier trail status before relying on them.
