# GPT34H–35: Chile Chico, Jeinimeni, Parque Patagonia

*Scouting report by a research subagent, 22 Nov 2026. Source: GPT Hikers Manual (Oct 2025) plus web sources listed below. Verdicts and figures are the agent's; verify before relying on them.*

## Area: Chile Chico, Jeinimeni and PN Patagonia (GPT34H, GPT35)

The manual's GPT35 detail pages (pp. 783–786) are marked "To be updated". They give only the section summary, an overview satellite map and the elevation profile; the km figures in the profile are on p. 785. The commentary is on pp. 417–418. Its line "Lago Jeinimeni to Casa de Piedra (km 99–154), Siete Lagunas" is a typo: km 99–154 is Casa de Piedra → Cochrane. To fill the gaps I used dated hiker reports on the wikiexplora GPT35 page, OSM geometry and SRTM elevations.

### Meseta (plateau) lagoons from road X-753 — verdict: ADD
- **GPT ref:** GPT35 regular route, km ≈41 → ≈30.6, walked northbound and back. Manual pp. 417–418, profile p. 785. GPT35 has Attraction 5/5. Reports describe guanacos, flamingos on the plateau lagoons, condors and foxes.
- **Car access:** X-753 (the Jeinimeni road) at about **-46.7913, -71.8979**. That is roughly 45–50 km from Chile Chico, past the Piedra Clavada turnoff (km 25). It is about 15 km before the park portal. The mapped fords on X-753 are near the lake, at -46.852/-72.009 and -46.861/-72.029, so this spot is probably reached without fording (from OSM, low–medium confidence). The road is gravel and rough; high clearance is advisable.
- **Hike:** out-and-back. About 3 km on a track, then a path, then open steppe up to the lagoon area near GPT "water 30.6" (S46.713, W71.886, ≈1,480 m). That is **≈21 km and ≈800 m ascent, 6–7 h**. You can extend to the 1,550–1,600 m plateau at about km 12.5 from the road (≈25 km, ≈900 m). Start at 770 m, about 1,030 m at km 4.5, about 1,300 m at km 8.5 (the settler/camp at GPT km 32.6).
- **Terrain:** a track and a faint path, then easy open cross-country. No fords are reported. Navigation is easy but needs GPS (the GPT track). Hazards are **extreme wind** (the manual warns about this stretch), clay mud after rain and a few fence or gate crossings. There is no exposure. Snow only from a storm; in a cold spell it can still lie at 1,600 m.
- **Fees / private land / permits:** The land is settler grazing land; the puesto (settler's outpost) is around GPT km 32.6. Hikers 2022–2026 describe the settlers as friendly. One report mentions a locked gate with an unlocked pedestrian door, but that was at the Chile Chico end. I could not tell whether the upper plateau is inside the Jeinimeni sector; if it is, off-trail use technically needs a CONAF permit (manual line ≈3100). No fee is known.
- **Current status:** wikiexplora reports from Dec 2025, Jan 2026 and Feb 2026 had GPT hikers crossing this plateau and calling it a highlight. There are no closure reports.
- **Why it's worth it:** This is the only real open-steppe and wildlife hike in the area. It is completely different from the lakeshore walk at Jeinimeni and the Piedra Clavada loop, and it has big views to Lago General Carrera and the Jeinimeni peaks.
- **Fits:** Plan 2, Feb 13 (Chile Chico buffer) — a direct replacement. Plan 1 has no free day at Chile Chico: either swap it for Piedra Clavada on Feb 15 (and do Piedra Clavada on the Feb 14 arrival afternoon), or use Feb 18 by delaying the ferry. Plans 3 and 5: only by dropping a buffer elsewhere. Extra driving is about 100 km return on gravel.
- **Confidence:** medium. Uncertain: whether the parking spot is on public land, whether a gate blocks the track at the road, and the exact location of the flamingo lagoons. They are reported "on the climb" and "near the top", but the reports give no km.

### Jeinimeni → Valle Hermoso, one night (Aviles traverse start) — verdict: MAYBE
- **GPT ref:** GPT35 regular route km 53 → 69 (Portal Jeinimeni → Raleigh camp km 61.7 → Lago Verde → Refugio Valle Hermoso km 69), pp. 417–418.
- **Car access:** Portal Jeinimeni / El Silencio, about 60–65 km from Chile Chico on X-753. The road is in poor condition with fords in the last section, and 4x4 is recommended.
- **Hike:** one night. About 16 km each way with roughly 300–450 m of ascent each way. Optional on day 2: a side trip to a glacier lagoon in Estero Ventisquero, which reports say is half a day or less and has "many easy fords".
- **Terrain:** a marked trail. There is a bridge at km 58.9, the ford at km 63.4 was dry and the one at km 66.1 was knee-deep (2023–2024 reports). Valle Hermoso has many small, cold fords; bring sandals. After rain these rose to waist-deep (Feb 2018, Dec 2021), and one report says the Jeinimeni crossing "between the two lakes" was thigh-deep (Nov 2022). Beyond km 69 the fords reach mid-thigh to crotch-deep and the trail has fallen trees, so **turn around at km 69**.
- **Fees / permits:** park entry plus registration with the rangers. Camping only at designated refugio sites; older reports quote about CLP 10,000 per site. Raleigh and El Silencio are not always open.
- **Current status:** CONAF listed Jeinimeni as "day visits only, El Silencio camping closed" as of the Nov 2026 page, probably a pre-season state. Hikers did the traverse Dec 2025 – Feb 2026. One Jan 2026 report says the trail had been swept away near S46.8897 W72.1647 and hikers used an OSM detour.
- **Why:** the existing Lago Verde day already shows the lake. Valle Hermoso with its hanging glaciers is the real scenery of the Aviles traverse.
- **Fits:** Plan 2, Feb 12–13 (Jeinimeni plus the buffer); Plan 5 only if a day is added.
- **Confidence:** low–medium. River levels vary from day to day, whether you can stay overnight depends on the rangers, and the ford depth on day 2 depends on that day's melt.

### Upper Valle Aviles beyond the loop, from Casa de Piedra — verdict: NO (MAYBE as a short one-night)
- **GPT ref:** GPT35 regular route km 99 → about 85.
- The planned Aviles loop (Komoot 16.3 km / 490 m, Andeshandbook 13.9 km, Gaia ≈14 km / 375 m) already covers km ≈91–99 and the suspension bridge. Going further means about 15 km each way just to reach the fords at km 82.6 and 83.4. Those were reported above the knee with current, and hikers had to link arms (2023–2024), which breaks your ford limit. Wild camping is reportedly allowed only above the suspension bridge, and the old refugios at km 81.8 and 94.7 were gone in Jan 2026. It adds little over the loop. **Confidence:** medium.

### Siete Lagunas pass from Casa de Piedra — verdict: NO
- **GPT ref:** GPT35 km 99 → 111.6 (pass at ≈1,120 m).
- About 25 km and 800–900 m return, at your limit. The trail is unmaintained and overgrown; reports from Nov 2024 and Nov 2025 say "officially closed" or that you need to clear it with the park administration. Lagunas Altas already gives the same high-lagoon scenery. **Confidence:** medium.

### Chile Chico → plateau from the town end (GPT35 km 0–20) — verdict: NO
- From the road (km 8.1) it climbs about 1,150 m to ≈1,650 m, and from town about 1,450 m. The descent on the north side drops about 550 m in 4 km. Too much ascent; the X-753 approach above is the better way onto the same plateau.

### GPT35 Option 4 (Paso Roballos) and other Argentine-side options — verdict: NO
- These are long, remote exploration routes for multi-day hikers. There is no worthwhile day out-and-back from a car point. Plan 3 only drives the Paso Roballos road.

### Things to know, and problems in the existing plans
- **Ferry (GPT34H):** The manual says two round trips a day and to buy tickets as soon as you arrive; in busy season or wind you can wait one or two days. Cars need an **advance reservation**. Operator: Somarco, Chile Chico office +56 9 4007 4902 and contacto@somarco.cl; barcazas.cl is the booking site the Nomades blog points to (updated Aug 2026). Residents get priority, and 2019 fares were about CLP 19,750 per car and CLP 2,300 per person. A new ferry, "Capitán Wellmann", was announced in June 2026. Book the crossing for the plan's date now, and keep the buffer day after it (Plans 1, 2, 3 and 5 already have one).
- **Fees and campgrounds:** Foreign adults pay about **CLP 9,000–11,400 per person per day** (blog 2025–26; GPT hikers 2024–26). Pay online at pasesparques.cl, which does not refund; a Jeinimeni ticket bought there is valid only for that sector. West Winds and Casa de Piedra cost about **CLP 10,000 per person per night, no reservation** (blog updated May 2026; reports CLP 8,000–10,000). Inside the park sleeping is only allowed at campgrounds, so expect about CLP 20,000 a night for the two of them.
- **Opening hours:** Pasesparques lists the Chacabuco sector as **Tuesday–Sunday 08:30–18:00, last entry 17:30**, while a blog says daily. Check this. If it is closed on Mondays, Plan 3 (Mon Feb 2, Confluencia trail) and Plan 4 (Mon Feb 16) are affected; La Confluencia is listed as a Chacabuco-sector trail. Jeinimeni is open daily 09:00–18:00 in summer, so long days there must fit those hours.
- **Stove rule:** the CONAF page bans fires, grills and stoves. Presumably the campground cook shelters (West Winds has eight) are exempt; confirm before cooking at the car.
- **Piedra Clavada / Cueva de las Manos / Valle Lunar circuit:** listed as "**closed**" on the CONAF page as of Nov 2026, probably a pre-season state. Confirm before Feb. It is about 10 km per the regional tourism site (7.7 km / 500 m per a 2023 blog).
- **"Sendero Lago Verde"** is not an official name. CONAF lists the Jeinimeni trails as Costanera Lago Jeinimeni 11.4 km, Laguna Esmeralda 5.8 km, Mirador 2 km and Escorial El Silencio 2.6 km; doing several together makes about a 16 km day.
- **X-753 road:** 60–65 km of gravel in poor condition, with fords near the lake. I found no 2025–26 report of the actual fords' depth. They rise after rain and on hot afternoons, and rental insurance usually excludes water damage. Ask CONAF Jeinimeni (+56 67 221 259) or in Chile Chico the day before. The "≈120 km return" in the plans is right.
- **Sendero Río Chacabuco (Cañadón):** reported closed in Jan 2026, though hikers walked it after signing a responsibility form (Feb 2026). That does not affect the plans.
- **Bugs:** tábanos (horseflies) are bad in late December and January, and mosquitoes at Casa de Piedra.
- The other plan items in this area (Lagunas Altas 21 km, ≈1,000 m; Aviles loop about 15 km from Casa de Piedra; Tamango) are consistent with the sources.
