Patagonia off the beaten path

Alternative plans for Jan 27 – Feb 21 2027 from Balmaceda that favour quiet, less-visited valleys and parks over the famous highlights, with up to two optional one-night tent trips. Most plans avoid the busiest places entirely; one mixes in a few highlights for comparison, and the plans differ in how much long gravel driving they accept.

Plan 1 · 🇦🇷 crosses into Argentina

Quiet Aysén

Hidden lagoons above the Carretera Austral, two tent nights, and the empty Argentine steppe near Los Antiguos, with no long back-road drives.

Best for: You want quiet trails and a couple of camp nights, but would rather stay on the main roads.

Plan 2 · 🇦🇷 crosses into Argentina

Back Roads and Empty Parks

Long gravel roads to the fishing hamlet of Raúl Marín Balmaceda, Jeinimeni's lakes, and Argentina's remote PN Perito Moreno, back over Paso Roballos.

Best for: You love remote driving and are happy to trade some hiking time for places almost nobody reaches.

Plan 3 · 🇦🇷 crosses into Argentina

Quiet Plus a Few Highlights

Mostly quiet trails, but with Laguna Cerro Castillo, the Marble Caves and the Exploradores glacier view mixed in, and a tent night at Lagunas Chirifo.

Best for: You want peace most days, but don't want to go home without seeing the famous sights.

Plan 4 · 🇦🇷 crosses into Argentina

Steppe and Lakes, Unhurried

The cheapest plan: ferry out, two tent nights (the Argentine meseta and Lagunas Chirifo), long stays and no long back roads.

Best for: You want to keep money in reserve, drive little, and sleep out under the stars twice.
