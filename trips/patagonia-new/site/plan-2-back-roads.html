Arrange it when you book: it usually costs a fixed extra fee and takes days to prepare. Not every Balmaceda agency allows it. Chile (SAG) and Argentina both confiscate fresh fruit, vegetables, meat and dairy at the border, so cross with none.

Why choose it

  • The emptiest places in the region: Raúl Marín, Jeinimeni's back country and PN Perito Moreno have very few visitors.
  • A real loop: out through Los Antiguos, back over the remote Paso Roballos.
  • PN Perito Moreno's hikes are first-class and nearly deserted.

Trade-offs

  • The most driving (≈2,300 km, much of it gravel) and several full driving days.
  • RP41 needs a 4x4 and good weather; the Jeinimeni road has fords.
  • Only one tent night, and the most fuel and food costs (no shops in PN Perito Moreno).

Sleeping in the car on this route

  • Coyhaique: Campgrounds on the edge of town with hot showers. Easy to shop and do laundry.
  • Puyuhuapi: Small campgrounds in the village; CONAF campsites at Lago Risopatrón are next to the Los Pumas trailhead.
  • Puerto Raúl Marín Balmaceda: Small campgrounds and cabins in the village; quiet beach spots nearby (ask locally).
  • Valle Exploradores (wild camp): Quiet pull-outs along the Exploradores road (the km 26.5 pull-out is the Rodado trailhead). No facilities; take all rubbish with you.
  • Chile Chico: Campgrounds in town. Chile Chico is the sunniest place in Aysén.
  • Jeinimeni El Silencio: CONAF campground at Lago Jeinimeni (El Silencio): stunning, basic. Designated sites only.
  • Tent: Lago Verde (Jeinimeni): Designated camp at Lago Verde. No fire; stove only.
  • Lago Posadas (AR): Small village campground or hostal; fill water and food here.
  • PN Perito Moreno (AR): Only designated campsites (e.g. Lago Burmeister, El Rincón, El Arroyo); book by email about a month ahead (reservasperitomoreno@bancodebosques.org). No roadside sleeping in the park.
  • PN Patagonia / Cochrane: West Winds campground at the park headquarters (≈10,000 CLP pp, hot showers, covered cooking shelters). Sleeping in the park only at campgrounds.
  • Villa Cerro Castillo: Several cheap campgrounds in the village. Quieter: the shore of Laguna Verde on road X-727 (free wild camp, ask locally about gates).

Between these places, iOverlander lists free and cheap spots. Most land along the Carretera is private and fenced, so ask before you park. Wild camping inside national parks is not allowed.

CONAF's trails at Lago Jeinimeni: Costanera Lago Jeinimeni (11.4 km), Laguna Esmeralda (5.8 km), the short Mirador and Escorial El Silencio. Combine them into a 12–16 km day. From the El Silencio camp past Laguna Esmeralda to a camp at Lago Verde, back the next day. The Río Jeinimeni ford is ankle to knee deep in normal water but mid-thigh after rain: don't cross if the water is brown or it rained the day before.
