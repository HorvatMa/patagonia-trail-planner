Argentina with a rental car. You need written permission from the rental company and the cross-border insurance it supplies. Arrange it when you book: it usually costs a fixed extra fee and takes days to prepare. Not every Balmaceda agency allows it. Chile (SAG) and Argentina both confiscate fresh fruit, vegetables, meat and dairy at the border, so cross with none.

Why choose it

  • Almost every hike is one the crowds skip, yet the driving stays on the main Carretera Austral and short side roads.
  • Two very different tent nights: an alpine lagoon basin and an easy forest camp with huemul.
  • A taste of Argentina's empty steppe without the long Ruta 40 gravel.

Trade-offs

  • Chirifo and Los Pumas are unmarked or poorly documented: you need GPS tracks and good navigation.
  • The Puyuhuapi detour costs two long driving days.
  • Río Tranquilo itself is busy (Marble Caves hub), even though the hikes from it are quiet.

Sleeping in the car on this route

  • Coyhaique: Campgrounds on the edge of town with hot showers. Easy to shop and do laundry.
  • Puyuhuapi: Small campgrounds in the village; CONAF campsites at Lago Risopatrón are next to the Los Pumas trailhead.
  • Villa Cerro Castillo: Several cheap campgrounds in the village. Quieter: the shore of Laguna Verde on road X-727 (free wild camp, ask locally about gates).
  • Valle Exploradores (wild camp): Quiet pull-outs along the Exploradores road (the km 26.5 pull-out is the Rodado trailhead). No facilities; take all rubbish with you.
  • Tent: Lagunas Chirifo: Tent on lenga patches above the third lagoon. No facilities, no fire. Very exposed if the wind rises.
  • Puerto Río Tranquilo: Campgrounds in the village; leave the car here during the Chirifo night.
  • PN Patagonia / Cochrane: West Winds campground at the park headquarters (≈10,000 CLP pp, hot showers, covered cooking shelters). Sleeping in the park only at campgrounds.
  • Cochrane: Camping Calafate (≈6,000 CLP pp, kitchen and showers) or Camping Tamango at the reserve gate (≈8,000 CLP pp).
  • Tent: Tamango lakes: Camp only where the ranger says, near Laguna El Cangrejo. No fire.
  • Chile Chico: Campgrounds in town. Chile Chico is the sunniest place in Aysén.
  • La Ascensión (AR): Caburé car campground at the Parque Patagonia portal, with fire pits. Register at the portal.
  • Los Antiguos (AR): Municipal campground by the lake, with showers.
  • Puerto Ibáñez: Small campgrounds near the ferry ramp.

Between these places, iOverlander lists free and cheap spots. Most land along the Carretera is private and fenced, so ask before you park. Wild camping inside national parks is not allowed.

A quiet, unmarked route from the edge of Puerto Río Tranquilo up to a chain of lagoons above the busy village. Camp on lenga patches above the third lagoon and walk down the next day.
