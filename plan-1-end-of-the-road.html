Why choose it

  • The most variety of any Chile-only plan: rainforest, hanging glacier, granite spires, steppe, desert and fjord village.
  • Reaches the true end of the Carretera, which few visitors drive all the way to.
  • No border paperwork for the rental car.

Trade-offs

  • Many bases (9) and several long gravel days.
  • Villa O'Higgins and Tortel offer fewer big day hikes for the driving they cost.
  • Puyuhuapi and back is a long out-and-back.

About 3 km of track, then a faint path, then easy open steppe up to the lagoons at ≈1,480 m. No fords. You need the GPT GPS track. EXTREME WIND is the main hazard, and the ground turns to clay mud after rain.

Cost: No known fee. The land is settler grazing land and the settlers are friendly, but close gates behind you.

⚠ Parking is at about -46.791, -71.898 on X-753, roughly 45–50 km from Chile Chico and before the fords near the lake. The road is rough, so high clearance is advised. Pick a calm day.

Why: The only real open-steppe wildlife hike in the area, with huge views over Lago General Carrera. GPT35 is rated 5/5 for attraction.

Portezuelo Los Corrales from Puerto Bahamondes

Villa O'Higgins · GPT37H regular route km 7.6 → 15.1
Distance
Gain
Time
≈15 km return
≈950–1,000 m
6–7 h

A trail of varying quality that splits into several branches. Around km 10.5–12.4 it fades into vague animal tracks with several crossings of the Río Salto, which were knee-deep after rain. The last climb is steep loose switchbacks. The pass (1,159 m) is broad and open, with no exposure. You NEED the GPS track. Fresh snow is possible.

Cost: Free.

⚠ The route passes the Villa O'Higgins hydro station, which has a 'personal no autorizado' sign. The manual says hikers crossing the bridge without lingering aren't bothered (2024 reports were fine), but technically you are walking past a no-entry sign. Turn back if the Río Salto is above your knees.

Why: The only real high-meadow day hike near Villa O'Higgins, with glacier views north and south.

Sleeping in the car on this route

  • Coyhaique: Campgrounds on the edge of town with hot showers. Easy to shop and do laundry.
  • Puyuhuapi: Small campgrounds in the village. Queulat also has a CONAF campground near the Ventisquero Colgante trailhead.
  • Villa Cerro Castillo: Several cheap campgrounds in the village, a few minutes from the laguna trailhead, so you can be first on the trail.
  • Puerto Río Tranquilo: Campgrounds in the village. The Exploradores road also has free and cheap spots with views (see iOverlander).
  • PN Patagonia / Cochrane: West Winds campground at the park headquarters: the trailhead for Lagunas Altas, with hot showers and covered cooking shelters. No reservation, first come first served, ≈10,000 CLP per person per night. Sleeping in the park is only allowed at campgrounds. Check whether the Chacabuco sector is closed on Mondays (pasesparques.cl lists Tue–Sun). Cochrane has cheap campgrounds as an alternative.
  • Villa O'Higgins: Campgrounds in the village.
  • Caleta Tortel: Cars must stay at the parking lot at the top of the village. There is a campground at the village entrance.
  • Cochrane: Campgrounds in town, or the CONAF campground at Reserva Tamango (next to the trailhead).
  • Chile Chico: Campgrounds in town. The CONAF campground at Lago Jeinimeni is stunning if the road is passable.

Between these places, iOverlander lists free and cheap spots. Most land along the Carretera is private and fenced, so ask before you park. Wild camping inside national parks is not allowed.

Strenuous, non-technical SAC T3

Laguna Cerro Castillo (day hike from Villa Cerro Castillo)

PN Cerro Castillo
📍 Show on map
Distance
Gain
Time
≈16–20 km return
≈1,100 m
7–9 h

The best day hike in Aysén. A steep forest climb, then moraine up to a turquoise lake under the castle-like spires. You register at the trailhead, pay a private-land access fee, and there is a latest start time. Go only as far as the lake and the moraine viewpoint. Do NOT continue onto the multi-day traverse's high pass (Portezuelo), which is the exposed and weather-dangerous part. Turn back if the wind on the upper moraine is extreme. Rules (2025/26): start between 08:00 and 12:00, or you are turned away. Ticket ≈21,000 CLP per person for foreigners, card only or online (senderospncc.cl / pasesparques.cl). Buy ahead because peak days sell out. Register via QR code at the ranger station. The last ≈1.5 km to the lake closes at short notice in bad weather.

CONAF's trails at Lago Jeinimeni: Costanera Lago Jeinimeni (11.4 km), Laguna Esmeralda (5.8 km), the short Mirador and Escorial El Silencio. Combine them into a 12–16 km day.
