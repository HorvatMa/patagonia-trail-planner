Argentina with a rental car. You need written permission from the rental company and the cross-border insurance it supplies. Arrange it when you book: it usually costs a fixed extra fee and takes days to prepare. Not every Balmaceda agency allows it. Chile (SAG) and Argentina both confiscate fresh fruit, vegetables, meat and dairy at the border, so cross with none.

Why choose it

  • Every famous highlight of the region in one trip.
  • Three of the best day hikes in Patagonia (Castillo, Laguna de los Tres, Base Torres).
  • Huge range of landscapes.

Trade-offs

  • About 3,600 km of driving: many days with no hiking at all.
  • The most expensive plan, at about €960 per person (park fees, campgrounds and fuel all add up). It is still under your €1,500-per-person maximum.
  • Torres del Paine requires advance bookings and is crowded.
  • Very little slack if a single day goes wrong.

Fees include the Los Glaciares Flexipass plus an extra day (≈€260 for two), the Torres del Paine multi-day ticket (≈€95 for two, new 2026 rates), Cerro Castillo, Exploradores and the ferry. The free CONAF car spots at Administración and Pingo can save campground fees in Torres del Paine. These are rough figures based on recent prices, not counting flights or the car.

Sleeping in the car on this route

  • Coyhaique: Campgrounds on the edge of town with hot showers. Easy to shop and do laundry.
  • Villa Cerro Castillo: Several cheap campgrounds in the village, a few minutes from the laguna trailhead, so you can be first on the trail.
  • Los Antiguos (AR): Municipal campground by the lake.
  • El Chaltén: Paid campgrounds in town (sleeping in vehicles on the streets is restricted). Showers, and the trails start a short walk away.
  • El Calafate: Campgrounds in town.
  • Torres del Paine area: CONAF has free designated overnight spots for motorhomes at Administración and at Guardería Pingo (toilets, no showers); ask the ranger whether a car counts. Otherwise use the paid campgrounds (Pehoé, Río Serrano, Laguna Azul ≈12,000 CLP). Wild camping inside the park brings big fines.
  • Chile Chico: Campgrounds in town. The CONAF campground at Lago Jeinimeni is stunning if the road is passable.
  • Puerto Río Tranquilo: Campgrounds in the village. The Exploradores road also has free and cheap spots with views (see iOverlander).
  • PN Patagonia / Cochrane: West Winds campground at the park headquarters: the trailhead for Lagunas Altas, with hot showers and covered cooking shelters. No reservation, first come first served, ≈10,000 CLP per person per night. Sleeping in the park is only allowed at campgrounds. Check whether the Chacabuco sector is closed on Mondays (pasesparques.cl lists Tue–Sun). Cochrane has cheap campgrounds as an alternative.

Between these places, iOverlander lists free and cheap spots. Most land along the Carretera is private and fenced, so ask before you park. Wild camping inside national parks is not allowed.

The hikes in this plan

Strenuous, non-technical SAC T3

Laguna Cerro Castillo (day hike from Villa Cerro Castillo)

PN Cerro Castillo
📍 Show on map
Distance
Gain
Time
≈16–20 km return
≈1,100 m
7–9 h

The best day hike in Aysén. A steep forest climb, then moraine up to a turquoise lake under the castle-like spires. You register at the trailhead, pay a private-land access fee, and there is a latest start time. Go only as far as the lake and the moraine viewpoint. Do NOT continue onto the multi-day traverse's high pass (Portezuelo), which is the exposed and weather-dangerous part. Turn back if the wind on the upper moraine is extreme. Rules (2025/26): start between 08:00 and 12:00, or you are turned away. Ticket ≈21,000 CLP per person for foreigners, card only or online (senderospncc.cl / pasesparques.cl). Buy ahead because peak days sell out. Register via QR code at the ranger station. The last ≈1.5 km to the lake closes at short notice in bad weather.

A gentle valley walk from town to the lake below Cerro Torre. Scientists are monitoring a slope on Cerro Solo that is slowly sliding toward Lago Torre (2026).
